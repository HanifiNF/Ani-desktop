About ANIdesktop

ANIdesktop is a desktop app for browsing and watching anime through one unified catalog. Search across the providers you enable, follow the seasonal schedule, save titles, pick up recent episodes, and play them in the built-in player.

It started as a desktop take on ani-cli, whose simple approach to finding anime and opening episodes in a media player shaped the foundation of this app. We are grateful to its maintainers and contributors.
